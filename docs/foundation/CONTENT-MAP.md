# Phase 00 Content Map

## Brand message

### Primary positioning

> Zenticsys builds automotive websites, digital platforms, and custom software for businesses that sell, service, manage, and move vehicles.

### Homepage headline candidates

Preferred direction:

> We build the digital systems that keep automotive businesses moving.

Alternative:

> Automotive technology, built for the way business moves.

Supporting message:

> From dealership websites and fleet management systems to service platforms, parts ecommerce, marketplaces, auction software, and enterprise automotive products, Zenticsys designs and builds digital solutions for the automotive industry.

### Brand proof message

> From customer-facing websites to complex dealer and admin platforms, we understand the workflows behind automotive businesses.

## Homepage content blocks

1. **Creative hero**
   - Centered headline
   - Short automotive positioning statement
   - `Request a Proposal` primary CTA
   - `Explore Solutions` secondary CTA
   - Automotive system visual rather than a traditional split hero

2. **Automotive audience gallery**
   - Dealerships
   - Fleet operators
   - Service centers
   - Parts businesses
   - Marketplaces
   - Auction companies

3. **What We Build solution gallery**
   - Dealership Websites
   - Fleet Management Systems
   - Car Service Platforms
   - Automotive Parts Ecommerce
   - Vehicle Marketplaces
   - Auction Platforms
   - Custom Automotive Software

4. **Workflow story**
   - Customer visits website
   - Vehicle/inventory interaction occurs
   - Dealer, service, fleet, or auction team manages activity
   - Admin/platform team controls the system

5. **Featured CarVu proof**
   - Enterprise automotive platform
   - Online buy/sell
   - Public and dealer auctions
   - Role-based dealer dashboard
   - Super-admin platform management
   - Only publish details approved by the team

6. **Automotive engineering capabilities**
   - Role-based portals
   - Multi-tenant platforms
   - Inventory and listing workflows
   - Auction and bidding workflows
   - Fleet operations
   - Service scheduling
   - Ecommerce and payments
   - API integrations
   - Reporting and analytics

7. **Proof**
   - Approved testimonials
   - Approved project metrics
   - Product screenshots
   - Client/project logos where permitted

8. **Insights preview**
   - Automotive-specific articles only

9. **Final conversion CTA**
   - “Have an automotive project in motion?”
   - `Request a Proposal`
   - `Schedule a Call`

## Global CTA language

Primary:

- Request a Proposal

Secondary:

- Schedule a Call
- Explore Solutions
- See How We Built It
- Write Us a Message

Avoid generic repeated CTAs such as “Learn More” where a more specific action is possible.

## Content tone

- Specific rather than vague
- Confident rather than boastful
- Technical enough to establish credibility
- Plain enough for a dealership or business owner
- Focused on workflows and outcomes
- No unsupported claims, invented metrics, or fake client results
- No broad “we build anything for every industry” wording
