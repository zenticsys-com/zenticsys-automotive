# Phase 00 Page Outlines

These outlines define what each page must accomplish. They are content contracts, not final copy.

## Homepage `/`

### Goal

Make the visitor understand Zenticsys's automotive focus and choose a relevant project path within seconds.

### Required sections

- Centered creative hero
- Automotive business-type gallery
- Solutions gallery
- Workflow story
- CarVu feature
- Automotive capabilities
- Proof/testimonials/metrics
- Insights preview
- Proposal CTA

### Required conversion paths

- Request a Proposal
- Explore Solutions
- Schedule a Call

## Solutions listing `/solutions`

### Goal

Let visitors visually recognize the type of automotive system they need.

### Structure

- Large centered title and short intro
- YNA-inspired large image-led cards
- Minimal title/description/CTA per card
- Featured solutions receive larger visual treatment
- No technology stack on cards
- No dense tags or specification tables on cards

## Solution detail `/solutions/[solution]`

### Goal

Help a qualified buyer understand how Zenticsys could solve their specific automotive problem.

### Structure

- Hero and audience
- Business problem
- What the solution includes
- Feature/capability sections
- User workflow
- Product/UI visual
- Integrations and technical capability
- Benefits/outcomes
- Delivery process
- Related case study
- FAQs
- Request a Proposal CTA

## Services listing `/services`

### Goal

Explain the delivery capabilities behind the automotive solutions.

### Structure

- Short automotive-focused introduction
- Image-led service cards
- Design, development, mobile, integrations, maintenance
- Link to relevant solutions and case studies

## Service detail `/services/[service]`

### Goal

Capture visitors searching for a delivery capability while keeping automotive relevance.

### Structure

- Service-specific hero
- When automotive businesses need this service
- Deliverables
- Approach/process
- Automotive examples
- Related solutions
- FAQ
- Proposal CTA

## Case studies listing `/case-studies`

### Goal

Create immediate visual interest and invite visitors into the work.

### Structure

- Large centered page introduction
- One large case card at a time or a deliberate large-card sequence
- Original image/product visual
- Title over image
- Minimal metadata
- One CTA: `See How We Built It`

## Case study detail `/case-studies/[slug]`

### Goal

Prove that Zenticsys can understand and build complex automotive workflows.

### Structure

- Visual hero
- Project facts
- Challenge
- Users and roles
- Workflow diagrams
- What was built
- Product screenshots
- Technical depth
- Results
- Approved testimonial
- Related work
- Proposal CTA

The hero may be centered, but long-form content must use readable left-aligned editorial layouts, columns, visuals, metrics, and optional in-page navigation.

## Insights listing `/insights`

### Goal

Build authority and attract automotive project research traffic.

### Structure

- Large editorial heading
- Automotive image-led article cards
- Short titles and dates/categories where useful
- No noisy metadata blocks
- Link to full article

## Insight detail `/insights/[slug]`

### Goal

Provide genuinely useful automotive technology content and support SEO.

### Structure

- Title and introduction
- Author/date/category
- Table of contents for long articles
- Long-form content with clear H2/H3 sections
- Original diagrams/screenshots where appropriate
- Related articles
- Contextual proposal CTA
- Article structured data

## About `/about`

### Goal

Build trust around automotive experience, product engineering, and long-term partnership.

### Structure

- Automotive-focused story
- Why automotive
- Experience with complex workflows
- Engineering/design approach
- Values/team
- CarVu or approved proof reference
- Proposal CTA

## Request a Proposal `/request-a-proposal`

### Goal

Qualify a project while making the visitor feel guided and understood.

### Steps

1. Project type
2. Business details
3. Goals, users, features, integrations, and existing systems
4. Timeline and budget
5. Contact details and optional links/files

Use conditional questions and a progress indicator. Do not ask every possible question on the first screen.

## Schedule a Call `/schedule-a-call`

### Goal

Let a visitor book a 30-minute Google Meet consultation through Calendly.

### Content

- Short “Are we a fit?” introduction
- Calendly booking experience
- Google Meet explanation
- Expected response/meeting behavior
- Alternative `Request a Proposal` and `Write a Message` paths

## Contact `/contact`

### Goal

Give early-stage visitors a low-commitment way to reach the team.

### Content

- Short message form
- Email
- Phone/WhatsApp
- Request Proposal CTA
- Schedule Call CTA

Do not add a live office clock or office-hours indicator.
