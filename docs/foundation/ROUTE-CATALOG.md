# Phase 00 Route Catalog

This is the proposed public route map for the automotive website. Every route must have a clear audience, search intent, and conversion path.

## Primary routes

| Route | Purpose | Primary audience | Page type |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/` | Introduce Zenticsys and direct visitors to the right automotive path | All prospects | Homepage |
| `/solutions` | Visual discovery of what Zenticsys builds | All automotive businesses | Listing |
| `/services` | Explain the capabilities used to deliver solutions | Prospects comparing vendors | Listing |
| `/case-studies` | Show proof through selected projects | High-intent prospects | Listing |
| `/insights` | Build automotive technology authority and search visibility | Prospects researching | Listing |
| `/about` | Explain the automotive focus and engineering approach | Trust-focused prospects | Informational |
| `/request-a-proposal` | Collect a qualified project brief | Prospects with a defined need | Conversion |
| `/schedule-a-call` | Let a prospect book a Google Meet consultation | Prospects ready to talk | Conversion |
| `/contact` | Offer a simple message and direct contact options | Early-stage prospects | Conversion |

## Solution routes

| Route | Buyer problem |
|---|---|
| `/solutions/dealership-websites` | A dealership needs a modern, inventory-led website that generates enquiries |
| `/solutions/fleet-management` | A fleet/rental/logistics business needs to manage vehicles, drivers, maintenance, and operations |
| `/solutions/car-service-platforms` | A workshop or service chain needs appointments, service history, work orders, and customer operations |
| `/solutions/automotive-parts-ecommerce` | A parts business needs vehicle-aware ecommerce, catalog, inventory, and fulfillment |
| `/solutions/vehicle-marketplaces` | A business needs online vehicle buy/sell listings, accounts, offers, and moderation |
| `/solutions/auction-platforms` | A public or dealer auction needs bidding, lots, rules, payments, and administration |
| `/solutions/custom-automotive-software` | An automotive business needs a custom internal system, portal, dashboard, or legacy modernization |

## Navigation decision

Do not create an “Industries” route or broad industry pages. Automotive is the single industry focus. Dealerships, fleet operators, service centers, parts businesses, marketplaces, and auction companies are customer segments within the automotive solutions structure.

## Route acceptance rules

- Every public route has one clear H1.
- Every important route has unique metadata and a canonical URL.
- Every detail route links to a relevant conversion path.
- No route uses generic multi-industry language.
- A solution listing card can be visually minimal, but its detail page must be informative.
